Alongside my research and teaching, I develop resources for learning, explaining,
and organizing knowledge. These projects connect language, visual communication,
and tools that keep information linked to its sources.

## Le français qui vit

**A French-learning book for everyday life and university conversations.** Connected
stories, practical situations, grammar beside examples, and exercises move beyond
memorizing isolated phrases. Published word-frequency counts provide a separate,
measurable reference; there are no subjective difficulty rankings.

[Explore the book]({{ '/projects/french-learning/' | relative_url }})

## Math Video Project

**Mathematics education and UQAM programme videos.** One strand explains mathematics
through animation and examples. The other presents studying at UQAM, its mathematics
programme, and possible study pathways. Both belong to the same video project.

[Explore mathematics and UQAM videos]({{ '/projects/math-video/' | relative_url }})

## Conversation Archive

**From scattered conversations to a traceable knowledge archive.** An experimental
extraction and organization workflow preserves source evidence, proposes connections,
and makes room for human review and reusable corrections.

[Explore the extraction project and its public preview]({{ '/projects/conversation-archive/' | relative_url }})
