## Mathematics and UQAM: two strands of one project

The **Math Video Project** combines mathematics education with videos about
studying at **Université du Québec à Montréal (UQAM)**. It is not only a collection
of mathematics lessons: programme presentation, recruitment and orientation are
also part of its purpose.

[Explore the public repository](https://github.com/xia-geom/math_video_project)

## Mathematics through animation

The teaching strand uses examples, counterexamples and progressive visual
explanations. Its curriculum covers algebra, functions and graphs, counting,
vectors, matrices, geometry and trigonometry. A separate collection explores
common mathematical errors and the reasoning that corrects them.

The current curriculum is organized into **27 numbered lessons and six common-error
lessons**. These are source and curriculum counts, not a claim that every final
render is publicly available.

## UQAM programmes, recruitment and orientation

The UQAM strand includes three complementary formats:

- **Programme and study pathways:** a longer presentation of the mathematics
  bachelor's programme and its pathways.
- **Studying mathematics at UQAM:** a recruitment-oriented introduction to the
  programme and its environment.
- **Interdisciplinary possibilities:** a short presentation of combining
  mathematics or statistics with study in another discipline.

[Programme pathways — project files](https://github.com/xia-geom/math_video_project/tree/main/miscellaneous/uqam-baccalaureat-mathematiques-cheminements)

## A shared production approach

The mathematics lessons and short UQAM films use Manim animation with optional
Azure narration; the longer pathway film has its own production setup. The
repository brings together scripts, scene sources, captions, visual assets and
render-review tools.

The same production project also hosts an
[English captioned introduction to Conversation Archive]({{ '/projects/conversation-archive/' | relative_url }}),
connecting the video work with the extraction project.
