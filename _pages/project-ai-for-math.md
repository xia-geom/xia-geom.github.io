<details class="ai-news-method" id="ai-about">
  <summary>How these notes are maintained</summary>
  <p>An automated research assistant selects, categorizes and updates the notes from public sources. The date above records the last source check, not the last site rebuild. Coverage is selective.</p>
  <p>Source checking is not independent proof verification. Each note preserves the source’s attribution and qualifications; a competition result, a formalization of a known theorem and a new mathematical result are different contributions.</p>
</details>
