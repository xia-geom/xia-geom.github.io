Reported finite-time blow-up for forced Navier–Stokes

2026-09-08 · OpenAI

OpenAI reports an AI-produced analytical proof and Lean formalization of finite-time blow-up from rest under smooth external forcing, with finite energy. The stated scope is the forced alternatives C and D, not unforced Navier–Stokes regularity.

Author-reported result with released artifacts; no independent proof audit by this page.

Claude-assisted formalization of Fermat’s Last Theorem

2026-09-04 · Anthropic

Anthropic reports a complete Lean formalization assembled with Claude agents, following an established proof strategy. The contribution is machine-checkable formalization of an already proved theorem, not the first mathematical proof of Fermat’s Last Theorem.

Formalization reported by Anthropic; the checker has not been rerun for this page.

2026-09-16 · Lean FRO and SAIR Foundation

Stage 1 invites faster algorithms for eight computational tasks, with Lean proofs that submissions satisfy the given specifications. This concerns proof-checking infrastructure, not a newly solved open theorem.

Competition announcement; no claim of an AI research breakthrough.

ICIAM on discovery, understanding and trust

2026-09-24 · International Council for Industrial and Applied Mathematics (ICIAM)

ICIAM welcomes AI-assisted discovery while emphasizing reproducibility, attribution, validation and human judgement. It also calls for reporting unsuccessful approaches and maintaining mathematical expertise.

Institutional statement; distinct from individual opinions.

ICIAM Terence Tao

A declaration on AI and the aims of mathematics

2026-09-11 · Math and AI (Terence Tao et al.)

The signatories argue that mathematical progress should be evaluated through understanding and the transmission of ideas, not only solved problems. They raise concerns about attribution, exposition and the training of future mathematicians.

Collective viewpoint; not a unanimous position of all mathematicians.
