This project collects short visual explanations for French-speaking students, built with Python and Manim. The goal is to make rigorous mathematical ideas easier to follow through clean diagrams, animation, and narration.

## Audience

The videos are written for students reviewing introductory mathematics topics, including material connected to UQAM teaching such as MAT0339 General Mathematics.

## Topics

Current scenes include the Pythagorean theorem, sigma notation, functions, and a 3D cone-to-cusp visualization related to my doctoral work in geometric analysis.

## Tools

The animations are produced with Manim. French narration is generated with Azure Cognitive Services text-to-speech, and the source code is available in the [math video project repository](https://github.com/xia-geom/math_video_project).
